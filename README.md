Use this prompt for Phase 1 UI/UX. This is focused, not too broad, and based on your new research direction.

Design a clean, premium 2026 SaaS UI/UX for the Phase 1 MVP of an AI Creative Operations Platform for performance marketing teams.

Product name: MarketPilot AI

Core positioning:
MarketPilot AI is not a generic AI marketing super app and not a Higgsfield clone. It is an image-first, human-approved, Meta-first creative operations platform. The system helps marketing teams generate brand-safe static ad creatives, review them, approve them, organize campaigns, plan content, and prepare for performance learning.

Main message:
“AI creates. Humans approve. Data improves.”

Phase 1 MVP focus:
Only design the first version. Do not include advanced video generation, full automation, all social platforms, or complex AI agents. Focus on:
1. Brand Kit
2. AI Image Creative Generator
3. Campaign Workspace
4. Human Approval Queue
5. Content Calendar
6. Basic Meta Performance Analytics mockup
7. Creative Learning Insights mockup

Target users:
Small marketing agencies, DTC brands, social media managers, and small businesses running Facebook and Instagram ads.

Design style:
Premium SaaS dashboard.
Dark professional interface.
Clean cards.
Modern 2026 UI.
Rounded corners.
Glassmorphism but not too much.
Investor-demo ready.
Simple, practical, and easy to understand.
Avoid neon gaming style.
Avoid looking like only an AI image generator.
Make it feel like a marketing command center.

Color palette:
Deep Navy background: #0B1020
Dark Slate cards: #121A2E
Electric Violet primary: #7C3AED
Soft Mint success/accent: #2DD4BF
Off White text: #F8FAFC
Cool Gray secondary text: #94A3B8
Amber approval/warning: #F59E0B
Soft Red risk/error: #EF4444

Create these screens:

1. Landing Page
Navbar:
Logo, Product, Features, Workflow, Pricing, Login, Get Started

Hero headline:
“AI Creative Operations for Performance Marketing Teams”

Subheadline:
“Generate brand-safe ad creatives, review them with your team, organize campaigns, and learn what performs best.”

CTA buttons:
“Create First Campaign”
“View Workflow”

Hero visual:
Show a dashboard preview with campaign cards, AI-generated static ad preview, approval status, content calendar, and Meta performance chart.

Landing page sections:
- Problem section: disconnected tools, slow approvals, inconsistent branding, weak creative learning, hard ROI tracking
- How it works: Brand Kit → Generate → Review → Approve → Schedule → Analyze → Improve
- Feature cards: Brand Kit, AI Image Generator, Approval Queue, Campaign Workspace, Calendar, Meta Insights
- Final CTA: “Build faster campaigns without losing human control.”

2. App Dashboard Overview
Left sidebar:
Overview
Campaigns
AI Studio
Brand Kit
Approval Queue
Content Calendar
Analytics
Creative Insights
Settings

Top bar:
Search campaigns
Create Campaign button
Notifications
User profile

Main dashboard cards:
Active Campaigns

Approved Creatives
Scheduled Posts
Average Engagement Rate

Main content:
- Campaign performance chart
- Pending approval list
- Best performing creative cards
- AI recommendation card
- Upcoming scheduled posts

3. Brand Kit Page
Purpose:
This page stores the brand memory.

Include:
- Logo upload
- Brand colors
- Fonts
- Tone of voice selector
- Product image library
- Design template uploads
- Approved creative examples
- Forbidden words
- Brand rules
- AI style preference notes

Add a small panel:
“Brand Match Score: 87%”

4. Campaign Workspace
Create a campaign management screen.

Include:
- Campaign name
- Campaign goal
- Target platform: Facebook / Instagram
- Campaign status: Draft, In Review, Approved, Scheduled, Published
- Creative assets grid
- Notes section
- Team comments
- Performance summary placeholder

Example campaigns:
- Summer Sale Campaign
- New Product Launch
- Festival Offer
- Retargeting Ad Set

5. AI Image Creative Generator
Design a practical image-generation workflow.

Steps:
1. Choose campaign goal
2. Select product/brand kit
3. Choose creative format
4. Generate static ad variations
5. Send to approval

Creative format options:
- Square Instagram Post
- Facebook Feed Ad
- Story Ad
- Carousel Slide
- Product Promo
- Testimonial Post

Generated output area:
Show 4 generated ad creative cards.
Each card should have:
- Thumbnail placeholder
- Brand Match Score
- Predicted Engagement Score
- Buttons: Edit, Regenerate, Send for Approval

6. Human Approval Queue
This is the most important screen.

Show AI-generated creatives waiting for review.

Each approval card should include:
- Creative thumbnail
- Campaign name
- Platform
- Created by AI
- Brand Match Score
- Risk Score
- Predicted Engagement Score
- Status: Needs Review
- Manager comment box
- Buttons: Approve, Request Changes, Regenerate, Reject

Make this page feel central to the product because the system should not publish without human verification.

7. Content Calendar
Design a monthly calendar for planned posts.

Include:
- Month view
- Platform tags: Facebook, Instagram
- Post status colors: Draft, Needs Review, Approved, Scheduled, Published
- Drag-and-drop style cards
- Right side preview panel for selected post
- Button: Schedule Approved Creative

Do not focus on all platforms yet. Phase 1 should mainly show Facebook and Instagram.

8. Analytics Page
Create a basic Meta-first analytics dashboard mockup.

Include:
- Ad Spend
- CTR
- CPC
- CPM
- Engagement Rate
- Conversion Rate
- Best Creative
- Weak Creative

Add AI insight card:
“This creative performed better because the product was visible, the headline was short, and the offer was clear.”

Button:
“Generate Improved Variant”

9. Creative Insights Page
Design a learning-loop screen.

Include:
- Winning creative patterns
- Failed creative patterns
- Best hooks
- Best colors
- Best layouts
- Best offer types
- Recommendations for next campaign

Example insight:
“Discount badge + product close-up + short headline performed better than abstract lifestyle visuals.”

10. Settings / Integration Page
Keep it simple for Phase 1.

Include:
- Connect Meta Ad Account
- Connect Facebook Page
- Connect Instagram Business Account
- Manage team members
- Billing placeholder
- API provider settings placeholder

Important UI/UX rules:
- Keep the interface simple and practical.
- Use realistic marketing data and campaign examples.
- Do not show unrealistic full automation.
- Do not include advanced video generation in Phase 1.
- Do not promise competitor performance data.
- Emphasize approval, brand safety, and measurable creative learning.
- Make the product feel useful for a real marketing agency.

Output:
Create a high-fidelity desktop UI/UX wireframe with landing page and app dashboard screens. Use realistic SaaS layout, clear hierarchy, modern dark theme, professional typography, and polished components.
